EMBER PACT

An elemental card duel against Rook, the Ashen Warden

How to play: You and Rook have 30 life. Each turn your mana grows by 1 (up to 10) and you draw a card. Play minions and spells from your hand, then strike with minions that have waited a turn. Guard minions must be faced first. Play two cards of one element in a turn to wake its Resonance — all your minions of that element gain +1 attack. Reduce Rook to 0 life to win.

Click a card to play it · click a ready minion, then an enemy, to attack · Space ends your turn · keys 1–9 play cards from your hand.
